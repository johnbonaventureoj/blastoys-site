Privacy notice

Last updated 12 September 2026

Blastoys is a sole trader business owned by John Bonaventure, 6 Courtyard Close, Syston, LE7 2JU. We are the data controller for the personal data described here. You can reach us at admin@blastoys.uk.

01 If we contacted you and you don't know why

We approach UK haulage and logistics businesses about branded collectors' models. We do not buy marketing lists. Business contact details come from sources that are already public:

  • the Traffic Commissioner's public register of goods vehicle operator licences, published on data.gov.uk, which lists the operator name, correspondence address, director or partner name and the number of vehicles authorised;
  • contact addresses published on a company's own website.

We hold this because UK data protection law requires us to tell you where we got your details when we did not get them from you directly.

Want us to stop? Reply to any email from us with the word STOP, or email admin@blastoys.uk. We will remove you the same day and keep only the minimum record needed to make sure we do not contact you again. You do not have to give a reason.

02 What we hold

  • Business contact details: company name, business address, a named director or contact, business email and telephone.
  • Operator licence information: licence number, vehicles authorised.
  • If you ask for a quote or a mockup: the artwork, logos and fleet registrations you send us, and our correspondence with you.
  • If you order: order, delivery and payment records.

We do not collect special category data, and we do not hold card details — payments are handled by our bank or payment provider, not by us.

03 Why, and our lawful basis

  • Legitimate interests — to introduce a relevant product to a business that operates a commercial vehicle fleet. We have weighed this against your interests: the contact is business-to-business, it goes to a business address, it is low volume, it is relevant to what your business does, and you can stop it in one reply.
  • Performance of a contract — to produce a quote, a proof and an order.
  • Legal obligation — to keep accounting records.

04 Who we share it with

Only where it is needed to fulfil your order: our manufacturer or trade decorator, so your artwork can be applied; and our carrier, so the goods can be delivered. We do not sell or rent personal data to anyone, and we do not share it for anyone else's marketing.

05 How long we keep it

  • Prospect contact details: up to 2 years from the last contact, then deleted.
  • Opt-out records: kept indefinitely, because that is the only way to guarantee we do not contact you again.
  • Customer artwork and order records: 6 years, to meet accounting and limitation requirements.

06 Cookies

This website sets no cookies, runs no analytics and loads nothing from any third party. There is no tracking on it and nothing to consent to.

07 Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, ask us to delete it, ask us to restrict how we use it, or object to us using it — including objecting to marketing, which we will always honour. Email admin@blastoys.uk and we will respond within one month.

If you are not happy with how we have handled your data you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113. We would rather you told us first so we can put it right.

08 Where your data is held

Within the UK and European Economic Area. If that ever changes we will update this notice.
