Why a model and not a pen

A branded pen goes in a drawer. A model of the customer's own lorry sits on a desk, in reception, or on a shelf at home — and it stays there for years.

Customer gifts

Something your best accounts actually keep, with your livery on it, in front of them all year.

Driver long-service awards

Ten years on the road deserves better than a card. Their truck, their plate, in a box.

Trade shows and open days

The one thing on the stand people pick up and put in their pocket instead of the bin.

How it works

Five steps, about five to six weeks from the moment you approve the artwork.

Send your livery

A photo of one of your trailers is enough to start. Fleet numbers too, if you want them on the plates.

Approve the proof

We mock it up and send it over. Nothing is produced until you have signed it off.

50% deposit

Production begins. The balance is due before the goods are dispatched to you.

Made and boxed

Livery and plates applied, each model in its own gift box, ready to hand out.

Delivered

To one UK mainland address, in time for December.
